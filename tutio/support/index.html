Tutio Support

Email eminsit@gmail.com and you'll get a reply from the person who wrote the app.

If you're reporting a problem, it helps to include your macOS version, Tutio's version (Tutio → About Tutio), and what you did just before it happened.

Common questions

The capture hotkey doesn't open anything

Another app may already own that shortcut. Open Settings in Tutio and set a different one. Tutio also has to be running for the hotkey to work.

Where are my notes stored?

In Tutio's own folder on your Mac. They are never uploaded anywhere, and there is no account. See the privacy policy.

Does Tutio sync between Macs?

Not yet. Notes live on the Mac you wrote them on.

How does the clipboard list work?

When monitoring is on, things you copy are listed for 24 hours and then cleared. Keep turns an item into a note. You can pause monitoring or clear the list at any time.

I deleted a note by mistake

Open the Bin in the sidebar and restore it.

Can I get my notes out?

Copy from any note into another app. A proper export is planned.

Suggestions

Feature requests are welcome at the same address. Tutio is a small app by one person, so the answer may be no, but it will be an honest one.
