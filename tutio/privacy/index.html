Tutio Privacy Policy

Tutio does not collect any data.

What Tutio stores, and where

Everything you write in Tutio — notes, tags, categories, reminders and clipboard history — is stored only on your own Mac, inside the app's own folder. Tutio has no account system and no server, and never sends your content anywhere.

No tracking

Tutio contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. It does not connect to the internet in either direction.

Clipboard history

When clipboard monitoring is on, items you copy are held on your Mac for 24 hours and then cleared automatically, unless you choose to keep one as a note. Clipboard items are never transmitted. You can pause monitoring or clear the list at any time in the app.

Crash reports

If macOS itself reports a crash to Apple, that report is handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. It contains no note content, and Tutio neither sends nor receives it.

Children

Tutio is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date below.
